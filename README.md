# Neuxis Digital

Create the Neuxis Digital website based on the attached brief and accepted structure:

1. Home: Hero with brand tagline ("Build the presence. Create the system. Move the business forward."), visual model showing connected systems vs. fragmented agencies, core capabilities overview, 3-phase process (Diagnose & Architect, Build & Deploy, Automate & Scale), concept showcase / capabilities in action, and closing CTA.
2. Services (/services): Interactive breakdown across Foundation (Brand, Web, Content), Growth (Performance Marketing, SEO, Local), and Systems (AI workflows, CRM lead routing, Analytics) with deliverable breakdowns and problems solved.
3. About & Philosophy (/about): The Neuxis thesis ("One partner. Connected execution. Measurable digital progress."), operating principles, and long-term vision.
4. Contact (/contact): Interactive project intake flow with goal selection, timeline/budget chips, and form validation.

Design direction: Premium, futuristic, minimal aesthetic with deep black (#08090B), graphite (#15171B, #22252B), white and silver typography, and controlled amber-orange accents (#FF8A32) with subtle tech grids and micro-interactions.
